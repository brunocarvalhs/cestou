Cestou turns your shopping list into a smart, shared, budget-aware assistant.

CREATE AND ORGANIZE
Keep as many shopping lists as you need — groceries, a barbecue, a monthly stock-up — each with its own budget and products grouped by category.

ADD ITEMS YOUR WAY
Search by name, scan a barcode, or just take a photo of the products (or a price tag) and let AI identify and add them for you. Pick from ready-made suggestions for everyday items too.

AI ASSISTANT
Ask the built-in assistant to help build your list, answer questions about what's already in it, or suggest what you might be forgetting.

SHARE AND COLLABORATE IN REAL TIME
Invite people to a list with a code or QR code. Everyone sees changes instantly, and members get notified when the list is edited or finished.

TRACK YOUR SPENDING
See your total spent per list, set a budget and get warned if you go over it, and review your purchase history once a list is finished.

MAKE IT YOURS
Light, dark, or system theme; Portuguese, English, or Spanish; and your currency of choice.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL
Clear the cache, wipe your local data, or permanently delete your account and everything tied to it, right from Settings.

Cestou works out of the box with no account required — sign in only if you want to sync across devices or share lists with others.
