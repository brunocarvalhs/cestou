# Feature Specification: AI Intelligence (Gemini)

## Problem Statement
The app leverages Generative AI to simplify item entry, provide smart suggestions, and assist users in making better purchase decisions.

## User Stories

### P1: Image Intelligence
**Acceptance Criteria**:
1. WHERE an image of a product is provided, THEN the AI SHALL extract the product name, brand, and category with confidence scores.

### P2: Conversational Assistant
**Acceptance Criteria**:
1. WHERE a user asks a question about their shopping list, THEN the AI SHALL provide context-aware answers based on the current items.
2. WHERE a user asks to add items via chat, THEN the AI SHALL parse the intent and suggest a list of products.
